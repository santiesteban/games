# Pushmo Challenge Packs

Two packs contain the selected Pushmo QR levels in the order supplied:
**Interesting Challenges** has the first 16 levels, and **Challenging
Challenges** has the remaining 15.

Each card was decoded with the Puzzle Maker's Pushmo QR decoder. The complete
decoded QR document, checksum, archive key, and image reference are kept in the
level's `source` metadata. Switches and paired warp pipes are mapped to Little
Ledges' native mechanics, including inverted pipe endpoints. Japanese titles
are translated for the game; source titles remain in the QR metadata.

To keep every level grounded, the creator's **Align to floor** operation is
applied to the decoded grid. The same vertical shift is applied to the flag,
switches, and pipe endpoints. Pom starts on the floor beside the leftmost
occupied cell. No puzzle cells or mechanics are removed.

The `パズル` entry is matched to its first numbered Puzzle QR card,
`zan-archive-086` (`パズル01`, displayed as **Puzzle 01**). For the repeated
`パズルブロック` title, the first matching card, `zan-archive-093`, is used.
The `zan-archive-050` QR title decodes as `パズルブロックS`; its translated
title is shown in the game, and the original Japanese title remains in source
metadata. Archive keys use the `zan-archive-<number>` format.
