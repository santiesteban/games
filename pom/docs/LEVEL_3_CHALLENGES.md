# Level 3 Challenges - P3DS

Challenges 3-1 through 3-5 are reconstructed from the supplied screenshots of
[the Pushmo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jO3XUbQJG7s) and the
walkthrough. The screenshots are small overview images, so the grids, pipe
directions and switches were reconstructed from visible boundaries and icons.
Display numbers 153–157 preserve the existing catalog numbers.

Challenge 3-2 is marked as having a known issue: the solver exhausted its
reachable states without finding a solution for this reconstruction. Check the
inverted pipe behavior and the screenshot against the source video before
removing that label. Other puzzles may still need visual or manual play review.
