# Candidate Challenges

Ten distinct source QR puzzles with no encoded pull switches or manholes, selected by ascending four-connected same-slot piece count from the reviewed Zandaxar gallery, excluding puzzles already in Japan Challenges. Exact payload hashes deduplicate alternate images; ties use archive key order. Full source decoder documents and provenance are stored in the catalog.

Following Japan Challenges, transparent outer margins were cropped to put the lowest occupied row on the ground. Internal geometry, colors, and goal position relative to the drawing are preserved; each piece starts at depth zero and Pom starts at the first occupied bottom-row column. This is an adaptation of the original 32 × 32 coordinates, not proof that the original game rules match.

A bounded Puzzle Maker solver examined each layout. Unsolvable means its reachable states were exhausted; undetermined means the budget was reached. No solutions or manual playtests have been verified. The unsolvable puzzle is marked knownIssues and is hidden by the default hide-known-issues setting.
